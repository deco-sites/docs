[`@decocms/parity`](https://github.com/decocms/parity) helps at both ends of a migration:

- **Capture** a live storefront (theme, fonts, components, and — for VTEX IO — the block tree + CMS content) into an agent-ready bundle with [`parity migrate`](/v2/en/parity/migrate).
- **Validate** the migrated site against the original with [`parity run`](/v2/en/parity/run).

<Callout type="info">
Full documentation lives in the **[Parity section](/v2/en/parity/overview)** — every command, the module system, focused runs (pixel-perfect, SEO, cache, vitals), configuration, and CI setup.
</Callout>
